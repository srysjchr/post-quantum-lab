                      If ek is public, can Eve run Encaps herself?

                      Yes. Anyone can encapsulate to a public ek and obtain their own (K, c) pair using their own randomness. That does not recover the K associated with Alice’s observed c. Bob keeps dk private so he can decapsulate Alice’s ciphertext locally.

                      Does successful decapsulation prove the sender was Alice?

                      No. Anyone can use a public ek. A KEM establishes secret material; peer identities must be bound by the surrounding authenticated protocol. Even a valid message tag identifies possession of a key, not a human identity on its own.

                      Is c the encrypted chat message?

                      No. The KEM ciphertext c establishes shared secret material. After key derivation, a separate AEAD packet contains the encrypted message, nonce and authentication tag. A public nonce is not a private traffic key.

                      What is visible even when secrets stay private?

                      Eve can observe transmitted bytes, sizes, ordering and timing. Depending on the network, she may also see endpoint information. Payload encryption alone does not hide all traffic metadata. This view is a teaching filter, not isolation from someone inspecting this browser’s source or memory.
